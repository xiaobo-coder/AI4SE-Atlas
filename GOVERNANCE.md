# Governance

AI4SE Atlas is maintained by an individual maintainer. Community members contribute through issues and pull requests; the maintainer reviews and merges them.

## Principles

1. **No rankings.** The project does not rank researchers, research groups, institutions, papers or projects.
2. **No popularity metrics.** Citation counts, h-index, stars or follower counts do not decide inclusion or order, and they are not displayed.
3. **Neutral order.** Researchers are listed alphabetically by family name (an optional `sort_name` handles names whose family name is not the last word), projects by name, papers by date.
4. **Evidence-based inclusion.** Every researcher, paper and project record cites at least one public source that supports its facts. The same criteria apply to everyone, including the maintainer.
5. **Suggestions are welcome, inclusion is not automatic.** The maintainer may decline records that do not meet the criteria in `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
6. **Corrections and removal.** Anyone may report an error. A person listed in the atlas may ask for their record to be corrected or removed; removal requests from the person concerned are honored without debate.
7. **No endorsement.** Inclusion does not mean that the project endorses a person or work, and the project does not speak for any university, company, lab or researcher.
8. **No private data.** Only professional, publicly available information is stored. Private email addresses, phone numbers and similar data are never collected.

## Roles

- **Maintainer:** reviews contributions, keeps the taxonomy coherent, publishes releases. Currently: Xiaobo Chen (see `CITATION.cff`).
- **Contributors:** anyone who opens an issue or pull request. Contributors are credited in `CHANGELOG.md` and the GitHub contributor list.

Nobody is listed as a maintainer or advisor without having agreed to it.

## Review cycle

Contributions are reviewed every Saturday on a best-effort basis. There is no guarantee of real-time updates.

## Disputes

Disputes about a record (for example, an affiliation or a topic assignment) are discussed in the pull request or issue. The maintainer decides, giving priority to the person concerned and to primary sources. Disputes about the taxonomy are discussed in a *Propose a taxonomy change* issue, and changes to topic IDs are recorded in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Changes to this document

Changes to governance are made by pull request and recorded in `CHANGELOG.md`.
