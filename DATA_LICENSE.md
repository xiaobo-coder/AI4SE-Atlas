# Data licensing

The MIT License in `LICENSE` covers the **code** in this repository (website, scripts, schemas).

The **data** in `data/` are different:

- **Facts** such as paper titles, author names, publication years, institutional affiliations and URLs are taken from the public sources cited in each record (for example arXiv pages and researchers' own homepages). These facts are not owned by this project, and we do not claim to relicense them.
- **Original contributions of this project** (the topic taxonomy, topic descriptions, project descriptions, record selection and the arrangement of the data) are made available under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Please cite the project via `CITATION.cff`.
- **Abstracts, figures and paper text are not stored.** Follow each paper's own license.

If you are the subject of a record and want it corrected or removed, see `GOVERNANCE.md`.
